Forever Recipe Finder 1.0.0-beta.1
For WoW Forever 1.60.1, interface 16001.

Put this folder in Interface/AddOns and restart the client.
Open with /frf or /recipefinder. Open a profession and click Scan.
Use /frf boxes or /frf bags for tradeskill crates and bags.
Not seen does not mean unlearned. Source hints and some requirements are unverified.
This add-on uses its own saved data. Disable the earlier ARL Forever adaptation if installed to avoid duplicate controls, then rescan here.
