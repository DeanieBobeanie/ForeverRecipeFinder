Inspiration
Ackis Recipe List, by Ackis, Torhal, Ressy and the ARL contributors, inspired the idea of a searchable recipe collection tool.
https://github.com/Ackis/AckisRecipeList
This project is not an official ARL release and is not endorsed by its maintainers. No ARL runtime, recipe database, translations, bundled libraries or artwork is distributed in this package.

Game data and sources
Recipe names, spell and item IDs, ingredients, skill thresholds, NPC locations and other game facts come from the WoW Forever Wiki recipe index, Recipe Registry, ForeverEra, Forever Talents, Eternadex and Zockify. Source URLs and evidence labels are retained in the data. Learning hints can include Classic/TBC fallback and are not a guarantee of current Forever availability.
https://wowfwiki.com/wiki/Recipes_database
https://theforeverera.com/
https://wowforevertalents.com/
https://eternadex.com/
https://www.zockify.com/
